Frequently asked questions

Is CTRACK a brokerage or investment advisor?

No. CTRACK is a personal investment journal. It does not execute trades, hold funds, or provide investment advice. The app shows market data for informational purposes only. Always confirm figures with your own brokerage before acting on them.

Where is my data stored?

All of your journal data — transactions, watchlist entries, receipt photos, and preferences — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad inside the app's private sandbox. Nothing is uploaded to our servers (we don't operate any servers for your journal data).

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no account. Open the app and start logging your trades immediately.

Can I export my journal?

Yes. Open Settings → Export and choose CSV. The exported file includes every transaction, with optional attachments bundled in a single archive that you can save to Files, AirDrop, or email.

How do I back up my data?

CTRACK relies on the standard iOS device backup that you already have configured in iOS Settings — iCloud Backup or a computer backup. Because all data lives in the app's private container, it is included in your normal device backup. For a portable copy, use the CSV export.

What market data does CTRACK use?

CTRACK fetches publicly available, delayed price bars and basic company information from third-party data providers so it can draw charts and populate your watchlist. The data is shown for informational purposes only and may be delayed or inaccurate.

Can I import transactions from my brokerage?

Not directly. CTRACK is intentionally a manual journal: every entry is made by you, with the notes and reasoning you want to preserve. You can always paste or type rows from a brokerage export into a transaction.

Does CTRACK send notifications?

No. CTRACK does not request notification permissions and does not push price alerts in the background. Any in-app alerts are visual and require the app to be open.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your device. Because CTRACK stores everything in the app's private container, uninstalling removes every transaction, watchlist entry, and photo. There is no remote copy to delete because we never received one.

Which devices are supported?

CTRACK is built for iPhone and iPad running a recent version of iOS. It is designed for portrait orientation and uses Apple's dark interface style throughout.

Reporting a bug or requesting a feature

Bug reports and feature ideas are welcome at berrakcantavan@gmail.com. Please include as much detail as you can: what you expected, what happened, your device model, and your iOS version.
