Privacy Policy

CTRACK is built around a simple idea: your investment journal belongs to you, and it should stay on your device. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data the app handles and what it does not.

1. Summary in one paragraph

CTRACK does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, share, or sync any personal data to any server operated by us or anyone else. Every trade, watchlist entry, receipt photo, and preference you create in CTRACK is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad using standard iOS storage. If you delete the app, that data is removed with it.

2. Data stored on your device

CTRACK stores the following information exclusively on your device:

  • The transactions you log (date, symbol, side, quantity, price, fees, notes).
  • The watchlist symbols and price alerts you create.
  • App preferences such as display currency and default chart timeframe.
  • Optional photos you attach to transactions (brokerage confirmations or statements), stored in the app's private sandbox.

None of this information leaves your device. CTRACK has no remote backend, no analytics pipeline, and no user accounts.

3. Permissions the app may request

  • Photos (read): Used only when you choose to attach an existing image to a transaction. The selected image is copied into the app's private storage and is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Camera: Used only when you choose to capture a new photo of a confirmation or statement. The photo is saved into the app's private storage and is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Photo Library (write): Used only when you explicitly tap "Save" on an exported chart. The app never writes to your photo library without that tap.

CTRACK does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, health data, advertising identifier, or any other iOS permission.

4. Network usage

CTRACK fetches publicly available market data (such as delayed price bars and basic company information) from third-party data providers so it can draw charts and populate watchlist prices. These requests are required to deliver the app's core functionality. The requests are made directly from your device to the data provider; no CTRACK-operated server sits in the middle and no identifier tied to your journal is sent with these requests.

You can use CTRACK in airplane mode for journal entry, review, and analytics. Chart data is the only feature that requires connectivity.

5. Children

CTRACK is intended for adult users managing their own investment records. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or under the age required by your jurisdiction).

6. Third-party services

CTRACK uses a small number of third-party services that are necessary to deliver functionality:

  • Market data provider(s): Provide delayed price bars and reference company information shown in the Markets tab. These providers receive only the symbol being queried, not your journal contents.
  • Apple frameworks: Standard iOS frameworks used for storage, charting, and image handling.

CTRACK does not integrate analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, attribution SDKs, social SDKs, or crash reporting SDKs.

7. Your rights and choices

Because all journal data lives on your device, you have full control by default:

  • Access: Open CTRACK to view everything stored.
  • Export: Use Settings → Export to CSV to download a copy of your entire journal.
  • Delete: Delete individual transactions, watchlist entries, or the entire app at any time.

8. International transfers

Because CTRACK does not transmit your journal data to any server, there are no international data transfers of your personal information to describe. Public market data fetched by the app is governed by the respective data provider's terms.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how CTRACK handles your data, we will update this page and bump the "Last updated" date above. If a change is material, we will surface a notice inside the app on next launch.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how CTRACK handles data, please contact us at berrakcantavan@gmail.com. We respond to every privacy inquiry.
